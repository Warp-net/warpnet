Emoji reactions replace the old binary Like. React to a post with any emoji, see who reacted, and get "reacted to your post" notifications. Likes, Liked by and the Likes notification channel are now Reactions throughout.

Hardened request signing: the timestamp and the destination peer are now covered by every signature, so stale or replayed requests are rejected.

Direct messages to bridged Mastodon accounts are disabled, so DMs stay inside the peer-to-peer network.
